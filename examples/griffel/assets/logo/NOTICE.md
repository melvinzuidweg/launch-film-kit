# Brand assets: not licensed for reuse

The files in this folder (`griffel-mark.svg`, `logo-mark.svg`, `logo-wordmark.svg`, `logo-wordmark-dark.svg`) are the Griffel logo and wordmark. They are © Griffel and are **not** covered by this repository's MIT licence.

They are included only so the worked example renders the way the real film did: `src/mark.js` holds geometry generated from them, and the end card assembles the logo from that geometry. That geometry is the same logo in another form, so the same restriction applies to `src/mark.js`. You may render this example to study it. Do not use the logo, the wordmark or the Griffel name in your own work.

To reuse the example for your own product, replace these files with your own logo, then regenerate the geometry in `src/mark.js` (its header comment explains where it comes from) and change the brand colours in `src/tokens.css` and the act files.
